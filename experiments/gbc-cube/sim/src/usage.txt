gbc-cube: Pokémon Crystal (or the demo cart) on the simulated 30 mm cube

  gbc-cube serve  [--rom PATH | --demo] [--port 3100]   web simulator
  gbc-cube bench  [--rom PATH | --demo] [--frames N] [--json OUT]
  gbc-cube shots  [--rom PATH | --demo] [--out DIR]     PNGs of the cube net
  gbc-cube snapshot --out DIR                           demo RAM for m33bench/

The ROM comes from --rom or GBC_CUBE_ROM; without either the demo cart runs.
Battery saves go next to the ROM (game.gbc -> game.sav).
